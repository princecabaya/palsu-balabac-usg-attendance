Student Attendance Portal Privacy Notice

This is a technical draft. It must be reviewed and approved by the appropriate Palawan State University office or Data Protection Officer before full student enrollment.

Purpose

The PSU Balabac Campus University Student Government Attendance Portal processes student information to issue QR attendance cards, record participation in authorized campus activities, provide personal attendance histories, contact a nominated person during an emergency, and prepare legitimate campus attendance reports.

Information processed

The portal may process a student’s name, student number, academic program, date of birth, address, phone number, emergency contact name and number, ID photograph, account status, sign-in security records, QR attendance scans, Time In, Time Out and administrative corrections.

Access

Students can access their own profile, ID files and attendance history. Authorized USG administrators can access records required for account administration, event management, corrections and official reporting. Attendance checkers receive only the minimum details needed to confirm a scan and are not permitted to browse private student profiles.

Storage and security

Records are stored in a protected Supabase database and private file storage. Role-based access, row-level database policies, private storage policies, hashed passwords, time-limited scanner codes, request identifiers and correction audit logs are used to reduce unauthorized access and accidental duplicate records.

Printed ID warning

Information printed on the back of the attendance ID can be read by anyone who physically obtains the card. Students should report a lost card immediately. PSU should decide whether full birth dates and addresses are necessary on the printed card before production.

Retention and disposal

Student and attendance records will be retained only for the period approved by Palawan State University and necessary for the stated purposes, applicable recordkeeping duties and resolution of attendance concerns. Expired records and files will be securely deleted or anonymized under the approved retention schedule.

Student rights and corrections

Students may review their portal information and request correction of inaccurate personal or attendance records. Attendance corrections retain an audit record showing what was changed, why it was changed and which authorized administrator made the correction.

Contact

Questions, correction requests and privacy concerns should be directed to the designated PSU Balabac Campus or University Student Government contact after the campus formally identifies the responsible office and contact information.
